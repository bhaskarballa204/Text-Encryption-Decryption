VaultTextTEXT ENCRYPTION STUDIO

Keep your words private.

Encrypt sensitive text with a passphrase, then decrypt it whenever you need it. Everything happens locally in your browser.

Security note: VaultText uses AES-256-GCM for authenticated encryption and PBKDF2-SHA-256 to derive a key from your passphrase. Your text and passphrase are not sent to a server. If you lose the passphrase, the encrypted text cannot be recovered.
